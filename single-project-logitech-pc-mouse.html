Overview

A mouse shaped around the hand, built for long hours

This Logitech PC mouse is a product design concept about comfort over time. Hours at a desk keep the palm and finger joints in constant contact with the mouse, so the design starts from the hand: an ergonomic line drawn along the side profile lets the palm settle naturally instead of gripping.

The back of the shell carries a special anti-slip texture of fine, flowing ridges. It gives the palm a comfortable support and helps reduce the discomfort caused by long-term use, while keeping the surface simple, soft and quiet to look at.

A dedicated Home button sits under the thumb: on Windows one click takes you back to the desktop, and on Mac it works as ⌘D. The concept was modelled and rendered in sage green, mint and sand, colours chosen to sit calmly on a desk beside a keyboard and a notebook.
