Overview

Kitchen faucet, rebuilt for effortless installation

Desliz Home came to us with a common problem: their existing faucet line took plumbers too long to install, and the awkward under-sink mounting hardware was a recurring source of support calls. The brief was to redesign the mounting system and the faucet body together, so installation time and the product's visual footprint could both come down at once.

We started with dozens of quick foam mockups to test hand clearance under a sink cabinet, then moved into CMF exploration to land on a brushed-finish body that would hide water spotting better than the old polished chrome. The final mounting mechanism uses a single tool-free cam-lock, cutting the average installation time from roughly 25 minutes down to under 8.

The slimmer body also freed up usable cabinet space directly under the faucet — a detail plumbers called out repeatedly in testing as the thing they'd tell other installers about first.
