Overview

A CGI showcase of material, light and craftsmanship

This project is a high-end 3D product animation created to showcase the design, material quality and craftsmanship of a modern shower tray. The goal was to recreate the product as faithfully as possible while presenting it with a clean, premium aesthetic that highlights its minimalist design.

Built entirely in Blender, every stage of the project focused on achieving a high level of realism — from matching the original surface textures and subtle material variations to refining the lighting and camera movements, with particular attention to the stone-like surfaces, soft reflections, and the integrated linear drain.

The animation explores the tray from multiple angles across all four colour finishes, bringing together product visualization, material creation, lighting, shading and animation in a single workflow — and a chance to push further into photorealistic rendering.
